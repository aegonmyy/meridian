    FX-4: WI-5 test integrity — real late-confirm delivery, tightened reverts (MUST)
    
    No contract changes.
    
    4a. test_wi5_lateConfirmAfterReconcile_isHarmless was tautological — it never
    delivered a confirm; it asserted a storage-cheat value equalled itself.
    Rewritten as test_wi5_lateConfirmAfterReconcile_isHarmlessAndSelfHealing:
    delivers a genuine CONFIRM_RECEIPT via a direct pranked-router
    hub.ccipReceive call after reconciling, and asserts (a) delivery does not
    revert, (b) inTransitAssets is unchanged by it (the id's tracked amount was
    already released), (c) spokeBalances[selector] IS updated from the message
    — the real self-healing property: a premature reconcile converges rather
    than double-counting once the confirm eventually lands.
    
    Extracted the CCIP-delivery pattern (_deliverConfirmReceipt/
    _deliverConfirmWithdrawal, previously duplicated in
    WI6_OutOfOrderDeliveryTest) into BaseHubTest as shared helpers, removing the
    duplication and giving every hub test access to precise delivery-order
    control.
    
    4b. test_wi5_reconcileTransit_revert_tooEarly used a bare vm.expectRevert()
    (would also pass on NothingToReconcile from a wrong setup) — tightened to
    vm.expectRevert(ReconcileTooEarly.selector).
    
    Audited the rest of the new WI/FX regression and integration test files for
    other bare vm.expectRevert() calls. Tightened one more: WI7's
    'exploit unavailable while paused' assertion now pins
    Pausable.EnforcedPause.selector instead of accepting any revert reason.
    (Two bare expectRevert() calls remain in test/units/hub/WithdrawPath1Test.t.sol
    and one in test/units/HandleRebalanceTest.t.sol — both are pre-existing
    tests outside this fixup's 'new test files' scope, testing standard
    OpenZeppelin/KEEP-class reverts where the codebase's existing convention is
    not to pin the exact selector.)
