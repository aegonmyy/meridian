    FX-6: spoke queue O(1) guard + recall-loop adapter try/catch (SHOULD)
    
    6a. _hasUnresolvedConfirms() linear-scanned the append-only pendingConfirms
    array on every setHub call. Added unresolvedConfirmCount, incremented in
    _queueConfirm, decremented in retryConfirm on success — setHub's guard now
    reads the counter directly (O(1)). The array itself stays untouched
    (history is useful). Updated WI-6's own storage-injection test helpers
    (_injectPendingConfirm/_markPendingConfirmResolved) to also maintain the
    new counter — they previously only wrote the array, which silently broke
    setHub's guard test once the guard stopped scanning the array (caught by
    the full suite run: test_wi6_setHub_revert_pendingConfirmsOutstanding was
    failing before this fix landed in the same commit).
    
    6b. Decided: wrap adapter withdraw() calls in _handleWithdrawalWithAmount's
    pull loop in try/catch. Min-capping already prevents the insufficient-
    balance revert, but not a protocol-level condition (paused Aave pool,
    frozen Comet market) that still reverts regardless of amount — without the
    wrap, that hard-reverted the whole recall handler, no confirm was ever
    sent, and the hub's withdrawal stalled exactly like the original Issue-1
    shape. On failure: skip that adapter's leg (new RecallPullFailed event),
    continue the loop, let the already-truthful actualPulled confirm report
    whatever was pulled. Extended MockYieldSource with
    setWithdrawShouldRevert() to simulate this. Regression test: two adapters
    funded equally, one forced to revert -> recall completes partial, hub
    correctly defers settlement (per FX-1's gates) rather than reverting or
    overpaying. Updated docs/revert-audit.md item #18 to reflect the closed
    residual case.
